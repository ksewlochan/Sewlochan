# Start Small

As you are starting out you do not have full clarity in where you are going.

As well you do not have a full understanding on how the financial world works and what is available to you.

So start small:
- I want to retire by age 50 with a million dollars.
- etc.


# Create a System That Can Evolve

Create a system that can evolve over time as you learn more and change your plans.
- Grab a notebook and on page write down your plan.
- Grab a word document and write down your plan.
- Use OneNote; create a notebook for Financials; create a section for Plans; create a Page for your current plan
- Whatever suits your style.


# Evolve

Each year or whenever a significant thig happens, update your plan.
- A million dollars is no longer enough to retire, I want ten million dollars.
- I get married and need to extend the plan.
- I have kids.
- Etc.


# Results

You have a plan written down.

You can update, add, change the plan if/as needed.

You revisit the plan at various points in time to actually make these changes.


# Things to Consider

When making your plan consider the following things (and more):
- [Financial Independence](./02h_FinancialLiteracy_LevelsOfFinancialIndependence.md)
	- When?
	- How much?
	- etc.
- Rate of savings
- Who are you planning for?
	- You?
	- Spouse?
	- Kids?
	- Other?
- Current Net Worth
	- Assets
	- Liabilities
- Expenses and budgets
- Emergency Fund
- Debt payoff
- [Investing](./02j_FinancialLiteracy_Investing.md)
- Estate Plan

But recall, do not get overwhelmed by the above list.  Start small and evolve over time.
